# 2-minute video script

**0:00 – Hook (Verdict section).** “Management wants to replace the ten machines that earned the least. Our dashboard says: don't. Nine of them are in the same quiet zone, and one is leaking.”

**0:15 – Luck (Section 1 scatter and histogram).** “We score every machine's luck exactly, using its bet sizes and its game's payout variance. The scores follow a normal curve, which is what pure luck looks like. Only one machine, M-1158, is far outside it: 3% hold against a 10% setting. Unlucky, or broken?”

**0:30 – Leak Detector.** “Luck alone can't tell bad luck from a broken machine, so we added a second test: how often the machine pays at all. M-1158 pays on 38.6% of spins against a 31.5% setting, every single month. That's a z-score of 124. Here's its Luck Lens: it drifts out of the luck band and never comes back.” *Demo: click the red dot.*

**0:45 – Location (Section 1 table, then Section 2).** “Look at the reasons column. Back Forty, low-edge game, short tenure. None of these machines draws less play than its neighbours. When the Back Forty walkway closed in March, demand there fell 25% while the rest of the floor rose. Traffic, not machines.” *Demo: set the Zone filter to Back Forty, then pick bank B-38. Point out the M-1120 → M-5076 swap that didn't help.*

**0:55 – Downtime and comps (Sections 3 & 5).** “Downtime is small, and Ridgeline machines cause most of it. The real surprise: on May 1, the point price of every comp was cut in half overnight. Comp cost per dollar of theo went up, and play didn't.” *Demo: click the May–Jun preset.*

**1:15 – Model (Model section).** “Trained on January to April, tested on May and June, with no future information. Our model cuts error by 76% versus a flat average. It showed the June promotion's lift was inside the noise, so we don't buy a $15,000 promotion weekend.”

**1:35 – Plan (Section 6).** “Our plan spends $15,100: inspect three machines, put up signs for the Back Forty, and run a randomized comp pilot. Each has a measurement plan. We keep $84,900. Drag the sliders and the plan stays positive in July. The GM's replacements would have cost $33,800 or more and fixed nothing.” *Demo: type 10 into the lease replacement quantity and show the checker flag 4 action types.*

**1:55 – Close.** “Luck, location, or a leak? Mostly location and luck, and one leak worth $73,000 a year, caught by an audit any casino can run tomorrow.”
