# Video script: "Luck, Location, or a Leak?" (about 1:55)

**Before you hit record:** open a new incognito window, start the screen recorder, *then* paste the link (https://deeps45.github.io/dashboard-royale-gilded-ace/) so the slot-machine entrance plays. Full screen, zoom 100%.

**How to read it:** talk like you're showing a friend something cool. Don't rush. The "…" marks are where you breathe or wait for a click to land. **Bold** words are the ones to lean on.

---

## Part 1: read-aloud script

> **[Slot machine]**
> So… the GM of the Gilded Ace wants to rip out the ten slot machines that made the least money. Before anyone spends a dime, we asked one question: is it **luck**, **location**… or a **leak**?
> Turns out, it's all three.
>
> **[Overview]**
> Here's the floor: almost **250 million dollars** wagered in six months. And nine of those ten "bad" machines? They're all sitting in the same quiet corner, the **Back Forty**. None of them is actually less popular than its neighbours, and every single one still **makes money**. A replacement would have to earn an extra 211 dollars a day just to pay for itself.
>
> **[Card game]**
> Go ahead, try calling it yourself. This one? Only seven weeks old. Not a fair fight.
> But this one… **M-1158**… is different.
>
> **[Lever]**
> Same game on both sides. M-1158 pays out on **38 and a half percent** of spins. It's supposed to be 31 and a half. Every month. In two separate systems. That's not bad luck; that's almost certainly a **misconfigured machine**, and it's costing about **73 grand a year**.
> And if you ran this check every hour, like we did in **Tiger Data**? You'd have caught it in **ten hours**. Day one. Zero false alarms.
>
> **[Floor map]**
> So why's the Back Forty so quiet? Watch March… they closed its walkway, and foot traffic dropped **30 percent**. Play dropped right along with it. It's not the machines. It's the **foot traffic**.
>
> **[Comps]**
> We found one more thing nobody noticed. On May 1st, every comp got **half price** in points, overnight. The points players are already sitting on went from **3 and a half to 7.6 million dollars**.
>
> **[Forecast]**
> Our forecast beats the baseline by **76 percent**, tested on months it had never seen. It tells us promotions don't pay here… and which July days need extra techs.
>
> **[Plan]**
> So here's our plan. We spend **15 thousand**: inspect three machines, put up signs for the Back Forty, and run a proper test on the comps. Every choice links straight back to its evidence. And we keep **85 thousand** in the bank.
>
> **[Ask the floor]**
> You can even just… ask it. Or take the one-minute judge tour.
> Luck, location, or a leak? Now you can actually **see** which is which.

*(About 300 words. At a relaxed pace that's around 1:55.)*

## Recording tips
- Do one practice run first. The second take is almost always the keeper.
- Smile while you talk; it really does come through in your voice.
- If the slot machine already played in your browser, open a new incognito window.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, check it's **2:00 or less**, and paste the link into Devpost.
