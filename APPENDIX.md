## Budget arithmetic

Cost = quantity × (unit + per-day × max(0, 30 − lead)). Inspect 3 × $1,200 = $3,600 (M-1158, M-1054, M-1210; 120 minutes of downtime each, under $100 of theo in total). Wayfinding 1 × $7,500. Comp pilot $4,000. **Total $15,100. Reserve $84,900.** For comparison, 10 leases = 10 × ($2,500 + 16 × $55) = $33,800, and 10 purchases = $220,000 (over budget).

Sensitivity: the signs break even at +2.6% Back Forty demand. Preventive maintenance ($9k) would need a ≥ 42% fault reduction on Ridgeline over 23 days. The inspections pay back if one fault worth ≥ $1.2k is found.
